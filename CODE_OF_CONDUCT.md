# Code of Conduct

We follow the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) principles: a welcoming, inclusive community where participation is free of harassment, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Be respectful and empathetic. Accept constructive feedback, take responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, discriminatory language, sexualized behavior, threats, personal attacks, and publishing others' private information without permission are unacceptable.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project publicly. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Responses are proportionate to severity: correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion. Reports are reviewed fairly and privately where possible; retaliation is prohibited.

Report concerns privately to @micheal081 using a private contact channel listed on [the maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/micheal081). If none is available, ask for a private contact channel without including incident details publicly. Do not use security advisories for non-security conduct reports.
